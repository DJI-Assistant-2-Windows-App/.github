## Overview
DJI Assistant 2 for Windows brings the maintenance tasks a DJI drone occasionally needs — firmware installation, sensor calibration, activation, flight-data review — onto a PC instead of a phone screen. DJI builds this application for its consumer drone series, and publishes separate builds for other product lines, including a DJI Assistant 2 edition for its FPV series aircraft.

## Why Choose DJI Assistant 2
| Without DJI Assistant 2 | With DJI Assistant 2 |
|---|---|
| Firmware updates handled only through a phone app and drone link | Firmware installed directly over a stable USB connection |
| Calibration issues left unresolved when the mobile app can't finish them | Guided calibration routines run from a full desktop interface |
| Flight logs stuck on the aircraft | Flight data exported to the PC for review |
